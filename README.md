# Data Professional Survey Breakdown: From Messy Survey Data to an Interactive Power BI Dashboard

Every year, thousands of data professionals share how much they earn, which tools they use, and how they feel about their work. But raw survey data is messy, inconsistent, and hard to read. In this project, I set out to change that by turning an unstructured survey dataset into a clear, interactive Power BI dashboard that answers real questions about the data industry.

## The Approach

I started with data cleaning and modeling: standardizing categories, handling inconsistent job titles and free-text answers, and building a structure that lets every visual filter and cross-highlight smoothly. Then I focused on design and storytelling. A good dashboard doesn't just display numbers. It guides the viewer from a question to an insight in a few seconds.

## What the Dashboard Shows

**Salary Analysis.** The dashboard shows average salary by role and compares compensation across job titles, so users can see how a Data Analyst's earnings differ from those of a Data Scientist or another specialist.

**Career Insights.** Job satisfaction levels and work-life balance are analyzed side by side, showing that pay is only one part of a fulfilling career.

**Skills & Tools.** Popularity charts show which tools professionals rely on most, from SQL and Python to BI platforms, along with technology usage trends across the industry.

**Demographics.** The distribution of respondents by country and experience level gives context to every other metric. It shows who answered the survey and helps readers judge how far the findings generalize.

## Key Insights

- **Salary distributions differ by role.** Data Analysts and Data Scientists follow noticeably different pay patterns, which matters for anyone planning a career path.
- **SQL and Python remain dominant.** Despite the constant stream of new tools, these two are still the core of the modern data professional's toolkit.
- **Work-life balance varies significantly by role.** The role you choose shapes not only your salary but also your daily experience at work.

## Skills Demonstrated

This project let me practice the full analytics workflow, from raw data to a finished product:

- **Dashboard design:** clean layout, consistent formatting, and intuitive navigation
- **Data storytelling:** arranging visuals so the story is clear at a glance
- **Business insight extraction:** turning charts into conclusions someone can act on
- **KPI visualization:** presenting key metrics clearly and concisely
- **Data modeling:** building a solid foundation in Power BI for flexible analysis

## The Outcome

The result is an interactive dashboard that makes it easy to explore trends in the data industry. Users can filter by role, country, or experience level and see how salaries, satisfaction, and tool usage change. It supports data-driven decisions for job seekers comparing career paths, for professionals benchmarking their pay, and for anyone curious about where the industry is heading.
